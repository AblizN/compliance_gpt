# ComplianceGPT

## What it is
A retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) system that answers questions over GDPR, HIPAA and the EU AI Act.

## Who it is for
Compliance teams who need answers they can cite, not just answers that sound right.

## Why this approach
Every answer is traced back to the official regulation text, and when the text doesn't cover a question, it says so instead of guessing.
